## Multi-key safety and compatibility

- Single client `proxyKey` is current model-route authentication (`server.js:1935-1942,4588-4599`); independent administrator cookie/session is separate. Legacy `X-Admin-Key` is accepted for model routes only. `POST /api/security` edits the single key (`server.js:4949-4963`), but `PROXY_KEY` env overrides the persisted `proxyKey` at startup (`server.js:1186`); a console rotation may therefore differ after restart. Must explicitly plan precedence/migration rather than claim permanent rotation.
- Account list is a destructive full-list save (`server.js:4796-4880`; `public/index.html:1292`). Add stable client-key ID ownership to normalization/validation, account draft, persisted schema, and safe projections. Existing accounts all default to legacy key ID, including environment-injected account. Do not confuse account's **upstream** `.key` with downstream client key.
- Auth check occurs in `dispatch()` before `handleChat()`, but the latter calls `acquireAccountLease(identity)` over the global pool. `enabledAccounts()` (`server.js:1347`) and all legacy/pipeline/cache-pool/stateful-binding paths need scope filtering **before** ranking, waiting, overflow or replacement. Stateful binding map is keyed by session fingerprint (`server.js:1227-1296`) and needs key-ID namespace/eligibility validation to prevent cross-key reuse. Do not add a second scheduler. Also review no-eligible/status decisions, `/v1/models` catalog, probe/test management exceptions, full-save invalidation, rotation/revocation in-flight policy, admin-password equality and every secret-redaction list.

## Performance hypotheses, not benchmarks

- `commitStatistics()` (`server.js:2567+`) prunes all 1440 bounded minute buckets **twice** per chat finalizer (`pruneStatistics` at `server.js:2489-2543` scans account/model/provider/usage/valuation cells). `record()` (`server.js:2739-2832`) serializes bounded ordinary rows, enqueues JSONL, then synchronously `saveMeta()` via atomic same-directory JSON rewrite. This is a plausible event-loop/latency hotspot as cardinality and metadata grow, not yet measured at 300 RPM.
- `JsonlLogGroup.append()` already has bounded pending records/bytes (`lib/jsonl-log-store.js:337-370`); no reason to invent another log queue. SSE first event, backpressure and keep-alive have existing owners/specs. Full/detail capture cost depends on mode: the archived synthetic 5 MiB sanitizer measured ~118 ms event-loop p99 on one local machine, not 300-RPM/target-container proof; raw 35 MiB capture is default-off and requires separate operational readiness.
- Test proposal: isolated temporary `DATA_DIR`, local mock upstream and one service process; reproducible 5 requests/s aggregate sustained window, plus bounded burst and non-stream / stream with varied response duration. Record achieved throughput, client latency p50/p95/p99, error category, event-loop delay, RSS/heap, CPU, ordinary-log drop/queue and metadata size; compare baseline vs optimized same machine/workload. Do not bypass per-account `maxRpm`/`maxConcurrent`, claim paid-upstream or production capacity, or introduce hard per-client-key limits.

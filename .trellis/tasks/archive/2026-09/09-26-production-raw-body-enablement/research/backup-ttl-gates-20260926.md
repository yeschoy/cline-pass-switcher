# Backup ownership and isolated 48h recovery — 2026-09-26

## Read-only host backup audit (not a proof of exclusion)

- Scoped read-only inspection found the live `data/`, `data/detailed-logs/` and `raw/` directories at mode 0700, `verification/` at 0700, repository root at 0750, but the parent `backups/` directory at **0775**; a prior specific release backup directory was 0700. This mode inventory does not prove every backup/copy is root-private.
- A bounded scan of **49 regular files** (at most 128 KiB each) under `/etc/cron.d`, `/etc/cron.daily`, `/etc/crontab`, `/etc/systemd/system`, and `/usr/local/bin` found no literal Switcher path/name and no explicit `raw/` exclusion. Root and ubuntu crontab listings were unavailable. `data/` resides on ext4 and is bind-mounted into the live container; installed rsync/zfs/btrfs clients do not establish that any copier runs. Historical backup copies lacked raw groups while production raw was disabled; that absence is not an exclusion guarantee.
- The scan cannot see provider/host snapshots, manual copy commands, off-host agents, other schedulers or future release-backup procedures. **Neither all ordinary/backing copy paths nor their 48h independent retention owner is verified.** Keep production raw off; obtain an authoritative operator backup inventory or implement and rehearse a provably enforced exclusion/expiry before raw enablement. Do not treat zero matching local files as evidence of zero backups.

## Exact-image synthetic store recovery

The committed-main candidate image `sha256:f7c45edc2ccba0eab83baea36db65ffe70e4a19fd4c1b275b8fa6565f4e765bd` ran `research/isolated-ttl-recovery.mjs` in a **fresh, sentinel-marked, otherwise empty synthetic DATA_DIR** as UID/GID 1000:1000, read-only root, dropped caps, no-new-privileges, tmpfs, cgroup 2 GiB and Docker `--network none`. The harness refuses an unmarked/nonempty mount. No host port or production body/config/key was used.

A strictly owned raw group with a timestamp **48h + 60s old** and a corrupt synthetic manifest was removed at the **first** store startup. A fresh valid raw group remained readable; the expired ID returned 404. A symlink using an otherwise canonical raw-group name was preserved, denied by `detail(id)` with 404, and counted as one `rawWarnings` event. After closing and reconstructing the store, the old group **remained absent**, the valid group was still readable, and the symlink remained preserved/denied; `health.failures=0` both times. The second construction did not independently delete the already-absent old group. This tests timestamp-based startup recovery and symlink denial in the target image, **not** real passage of 48 hours, minute-timer scheduling under live traffic, deletion after an I/O failure, service restart with full HTTP/auth stack, or expiry of external copies. The suspicious synthetic fixture remains in private verification storage pending cleanup consent; no production raw group was touched.

**Disposition:** a narrow 48h store-recovery gate has supporting evidence, but the combined backup/external-expiry and full rollback gates remain blocked. No live Compose/config/admin setting/image was changed in this audit.

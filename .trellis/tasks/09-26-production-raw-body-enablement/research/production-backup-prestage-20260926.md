## Mandatory next fence, not yet executed

The pre-copy is **not** the requested full accepted backup. The service must be put in an agreed maintenance window with a tested independent resume/watchdog path; only once writes are fenced may the copy be resynchronized, source/destination file bytes/modes/owners and complete tree checked, sidecars rechecked, and an isolated old-image recovery check performed. Never replace live DATA_DIR to demonstrate a restore. If a concurrent external write or hash drift is detected, leave the copy untrusted, resume old service and report a blocked gate. A live-scale verification may take longer than the 1.1-second pre-copy; that observation does **not** establish a downtime bound.

A **concrete maximum outage window and recovery procedure** still need operator review before stopping production. The partial and complete private pre-stage should be retained for the next fenced attempt, not promoted to rollback material or cleaned without a separate decision.

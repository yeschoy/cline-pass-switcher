## Synthetic recovery evidence and rejected assumptions

- A disposable no-network container was paused after a `systemd-run --on-active=5s` unpause timer was armed; **after SSH disconnected**, the timer returned it to running. Another disposable 600-second timer had a payload targeting the exact container ID and a `busctl` monotonic deadline 600 seconds ahead; it was canceled without pausing production. This validates narrow host timer mechanics only, not a 600-second live-service outage guarantee.
- Read-only host probes exposed three fail-closed local script assumptions: `/root` stage location, an absolute private `.env` path in Compose, and UTF-8 Nginx comments. They were corrected in local synthetic tests. The one-shot timer had a more serious pause-after-fire race: if the coordination process stalled after timer expiry but before Docker pause, the container could stay paused. The research helper was redesigned around a **persistent systemd-supervised exact-ID watchdog** that retries unpause after an absolute monotonic deadline and remains armed through ambiguous pause/recovery. Independent local review found the helper suitable to commit **as unvalidated research only**, not execute against the live service. The revised persistent watchdog has not yet been installed or exercised with real systemd/Docker, and the root-installed script hash/provenance is not yet established.

Final scans only detect writes through the last observation; a verified writer boundary and private full-tree seal/isolated restore are still required.

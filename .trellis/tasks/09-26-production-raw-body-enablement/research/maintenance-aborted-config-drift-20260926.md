## Safety work before the aborted boundary

- A live DATA_DIR pre-copy (~960 MB, 36,979 regular files at that snapshot) was made under the existing `verification/` path with raw excluded, plus private Compose/env/gateway/deployment sidecars. It remained `precopy-unverified/trusted:false`. Review found its **parent `verification/` is 0700 but owned by UID 1000**, not root; a root-owned child inside that writable parent cannot satisfy the intended root-private backup ownership fence. Do not promote or restore from this obsolete stage.
- A lower-risk backup-only **Docker pause/unpause** approach was tested on a synthetic container. A detached `systemd-run --on-active=8s /usr/bin/docker unpause <synthetic container>` returned that paused container to running after the SSH session ended. This proves only the specific synthetic timer dispatch, not the final production 600-second guard, live HTTP recovery or a hard outage bound. An unreviewed stop/start prototype was rejected before use. No watchdog was armed against production.
- The planned replacement root-only stage under `/opt/` was checked but **not created**. Immediately before creating it, a fresh SHA-256 fence found that live `config.json` no longer matched the frozen `e985edba49cf50aa25974db25accc53a9681e8964fcb05348854b9e889820731`. The procedure stopped before any new copy or pause. Do not weaken the fence or silently reuse the old v1 snapshot.

## Disposition

**Hard stop before production-control mutation.** The account and scheduling/detail saves were confirmed; subsequent hash drift, a root-private 32-account JSON-only rehearsal and a new **untrusted data-only** pre-copy are documented in `post-save-migration-20260927.md`. Investigate the marked fall in live detailed-log files before accepting the newer tree as expected complete state. Validate an independently supervised unpause guard, container identity, full-corpus timing, sidecar authority and failure recovery before considering a new, separately approved pause. A future backup-only pause must still be distinct from code deployment, and raw-on requires its own external backup/privacy/resource/browser gates. The stale private pre-copy and unreviewed local watchdog prototypes are retained but **not accepted**; cleanup requires a separate decision, not silent deletion.
